## The missing mathematical certificate

If ||gradient F50||_infinity is uniformly bounded by L on both connecting segments, then the endpoint contribution to centered-difference contamination is at most L*(||delta+||_1+||delta-||_1)/(2h). N1's native row gives one local gradient at x0, not either segment supremum. A baseline-position shift also changes the native derivative and needs a certified spatial-variation bound on that row, such as a suitable bound involving third energy derivatives. A finite-precision implementation additionally needs relevant arithmetic-error bounds; the differentiable real-arithmetic argument does not automatically certify the rounded program.

No such uniform derivative/remainder certificate is present. Finite force samples and a local derivative cannot establish it: smooth functions can agree at finitely many sampled values/derivatives while differing between those points. That observation concerns inference from finite samples; it does not replace the actual known model by a hypothetical function. A separate model-specific analytic or interval proof could, in principle, use its complete source and parameters. This stage has not produced that proof, and a small local projection is not a substitute.
